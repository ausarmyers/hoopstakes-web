❓ Quick Answers

How do I cash out?
Upgrade to Hoopster or Elite, verify your email, then tap "Cash Out" in the Wallet tab. Minimum: $10 (Hoopster) or $5 (Elite).
Why is my LEO score "Calibrating"?
New players (<3 games) see a dampened score to prevent early volatility. Play 3+ games to lock in your full LEO rating.
Can I change my position?
Yes! Your position is locked after 5 games to ensure fair matching. Before that, edit it anytime in your Profile.
What if my match score is disputed?
Both players must confirm scores. If there's a disagreement, file a dispute within 24 hours in the Match Details screen. Our team will review and resolve.
Is HoopStakes available in my city?
We're launching in San Diego, Miami, and Broward first. If your city isn't active, join the waitlist in the App to be notified when we expand.
