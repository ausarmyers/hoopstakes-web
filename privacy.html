🏀 HoopStakes Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

HoopStakes ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application HoopStakes (the "App").

By using HoopStakes, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with our terms and practices, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Email Address: Collected during sign-up for account creation, recovery, and cashout verification
  • Display Name: Your chosen basketball nickname shown on leaderboards
  • Age/Date of Birth: Collected to enforce our 13+ age requirement (COPPA compliance)

1.2 Profile & Gameplay Data

  • City/Location Preference: Your selected home city for court matching
  • Basketball Position: PG, SG, SF, PF, or C (locked after 5 games)
  • LEO Score & Statistics: Wins, losses, win streaks, average margin, games played
  • Match History: Date, location, opponents, scores, and dispute status
  • Tier Status: Rookie, Hoopster, or Elite membership level

1.3 Location Data

  • Precise Location: Collected ONLY when you check in at a court to verify proximity (within 50 meters)
  • Purpose: Prevent fraud, validate match submissions, ensure fair play
  • When: Only during active court check-in and match submission
  • Not tracked: We do NOT continuously track your location in the background

1.4 Device & Usage Information

  • Device ID: Unique identifier for your phone (used for account security)
  • App Activity: Features used, screens viewed, session duration
  • Crash Data: Error logs to improve app stability
  • Performance Metrics: Load times, API response times

1.5 Financial Information

  • We do NOT collect: Credit card numbers, bank account details, or payment credentials
  • Handled by: Stripe (our payment processor) for subscription tiers and cashouts
  • What we store: Transaction IDs, subscription status, earned balance (virtual credits)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Provide Core Features: Court check-in, match submission, LEO scoring, leaderboards
  • Account Management: Authentication, profile updates, password recovery
  • Fair Play Enforcement: Geofence verification, dispute resolution, fraud detection
  • Cashout Processing: Verify identity, process payouts to eligible users
  • App Improvement: Analyze usage patterns, fix bugs, optimize performance
  • Communication: Send match confirmations, dispute notifications, policy updates
  • Legal Compliance: Enforce age restrictions, respond to lawful requests

3. How We Share Your Information

3.1 Public Information (Visible to Other Users)

  • Display Name
  • LEO Score (calibrated if <3 games)
  • Win/Loss Record
  • Basketball Position
  • Tier Status
  • City (general area only, not exact address)

This information is visible to other HoopStakes users for matchmaking and leaderboard purposes.

3.2 Private Information (Never Shared Publicly)

  • Email address
  • Precise location history
  • Device ID
  • Financial data
  • Dispute details (unless required for resolution)

3.3 Third-Party Service Providers

We share data with trusted partners who perform services on our behalf:

  • Firebase (Google): Database hosting, authentication, analytics
  • Stripe: Payment processing for subscriptions and cashouts
  • Expo: App infrastructure and over-the-air updates
  • Sentry (optional): Crash reporting and error tracking

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and cannot use it for any other purpose.

3.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., court order, subpoena).

4. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active + 2 years after last login
  • Match History: Retained for 5 years (for dispute resolution and LEO accuracy)
  • Location Data: Deleted immediately after court verification (not stored long-term)
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated and anonymized after 90 days

5. Your Data Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

5.1 Access & Portability

Request a copy of all data we hold about you in a machine-readable format. Email: hello@hoopstakes.com

5.2 Correction

Update or correct inaccurate information via the App's profile settings or by contacting us.

5.3 Deletion

Request permanent deletion of your account and associated data. Email: delete@hoopstakes.com

  • We will delete your data within 30 days
  • Exception: Match history may be retained (anonymized) for leaderboard integrity

5.4 Opt-Out

  • Marketing: We do NOT send marketing emails
  • Analytics: Limited opt-out via device settings (Limit Ad Tracking on iOS, Opt-out of Ads Personalization on Android)

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

  • Age Restriction: HoopStakes is NOT intended for users under 13 years old
  • Age Gate: We require date of birth during profile completion
  • Blocking: Users who indicate they are under 13 cannot create an account
  • No Collection: We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13
  • Parental Rights: If you believe we have data from a child under 13, contact privacy@hoopstakes.com

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • Authentication: Firebase Authentication with email verification for cashouts
  • Database Security: Firestore security rules prevent unauthorized access
  • Access Controls: Limited employee access to production data
  • Regular Audits: Security reviews of third-party providers

Limitation: No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International Data Transfers

HoopStakes operates in the United States. If you access the App from outside the U.S., your data will be transferred to and processed in the U.S. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses for EU users).

9. Cookies & Tracking

  • App Storage: We use local storage on your device for session management and offline functionality
  • No Third-Party Cookies: We do NOT use advertising cookies or cross-app tracking
  • Analytics: Limited to app performance and feature usage (no personal identifiers)

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by:

  • Posting the new policy on this page
  • Updating the "Last updated" date
  • Sending an in-app notification for material changes

Your continued use of HoopStakes after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

12. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data is collected
  • Know whether personal data is sold or disclosed (we do NOT sell data)
  • Opt-out of the sale of personal data (not applicable — we don't sell)
  • Request deletion of personal data
  • Non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights

To exercise these rights, email privacy@hoopstakes.com.

13. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Union, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

Legal basis for processing: Performance of contract (providing App features), legitimate interests (fraud prevention, app improvement), and consent (location data).
